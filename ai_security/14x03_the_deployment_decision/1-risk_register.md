## Executive Summary: Stated Controls vs. Empirical Reality

An audit of the staging logs (`05-sample-logs.jsonl`), data-flow diagrams (`02-dataflow-diagram.md`), vendor contracts (`04-dpa-excerpt.md`), and the internal compliance self-assessment (`06-compliance-self-assessment.md`) reveals critical discrepancies between what the system is *claimed* to do and what it *actually* does:

1. **False Compliance Assertions (C-08):** The self-assessment claims all ingested retrieval content is source-verified and signed by `docs-ci`. In reality (`01-architecture.md` §5, `05-sample-logs.jsonl`), `ingest_source_policy` is set to `open`, allowing unsigned, untrusted `ticket_submission` documents directly into the RAG index.
2. **Active Indirect Prompt Injection (Log Evidence):** In session `ses_532fc830` (`evt_000241`–`evt_000246`), an unsigned customer ticket (`tkt-7576`) hijacked the agent's execution flow, forcing an unauthorized `billing_lookup` (`context_origin: "retrieved:ticket_submission"`).
3. **Asymmetric Data Scrubbing:** While `field-scrubber` redacts `iban`, `billing_address`, and `tax_id` *before* the model call (`model-adapter`), **there is no reply-side filter** (`F2` is unbuilt). Furthermore, `billing_lookup` returns raw regulated fields into the execution environment even when irrelevant (e.g., when a user asks *"How do I change my plan?"* or *"What payment methods do you accept?"*), creating an active exfiltration vector if the model is coerced or if raw tool outputs leak.
4. **Third-Party Retention & Human Review Exposure:** Despite the vendor model card claiming privacy, DPA Clauses 4.2, 5.2, and 6.1 allow Tessel AI and contracted third-party human reviewers to retain flagged prompts/tool results for **365 days** and use them to train safety classifiers.
5. **Approval Fatigue & Race-Condition/Ordering Anomalies in Logs:** Human agents (`agt_tosei`, `agt_mpatel`, `agt_rhale`, `agt_lduarte`) approve refunds in 5.6 to 14.9 minutes, yet logs show `issue_refund` being proposed/logged with mismatched reasons (e.g., user says *"cancelled in window"* while tool argument logs `reason: "duplicate charge"` in `evt_000142` and `evt_000179`), and `tool_call` events appearing *after* `model_call` or `reply` timestamps in several traces, indicating asynchronous logging defects or missing guardrails.
