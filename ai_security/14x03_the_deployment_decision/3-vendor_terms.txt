# VENDOR DATA PROCESSING TERMS & RISK REGISTER MAPPING
# Parties: Larkspur Software ("Customer") & Tessel AI ("Provider") | Model: tessel-core-2
# Enforceable Document: 04-dpa-excerpt.md (Data Processing Addendum, Clauses 3–6)
# Non-Enforceable Informational Document: 03-vendor-model-card.md (Model Card — explicitly defers legal terms to the agreement)

[ENFORCEABLE PERMISSIONS & RESTRICTIONS — 04-dpa-excerpt.md]
1. SCOPE OF CUSTOMER INPUT: "Customer Input" includes prompts, context, tool results, and any personal data contained in them sent to the service. | Source: 04-dpa-excerpt.md, Clause 3.2
2. PURPOSE LIMITATION: Provider may process Customer Input solely to provide the model service to Customer and as otherwise documented in the DPA (including abuse monitoring, service integrity, and safety classifier training). | Source: 04-dpa-excerpt.md, Clauses 3.1, 4.1, 4.2, 5.2
3. DEFAULT RETENTION (30 DAYS): Provider retains Customer Input and the corresponding generated output for 30 days after each request for abuse monitoring and service integrity, after which they are deleted (NOT zero-retention). | Source: 04-dpa-excerpt.md, Clause 4.1
4. EXTENDED RETENTION ON ABUSE FLAG (UP TO 365 DAYS): Where Provider's automated systems flag a request for suspected abuse, Provider may retain that request and its output for up to 365 days. | Source: 04-dpa-excerpt.md, Clause 4.2
5. FOUNDATION MODEL TRAINING PROHIBITION: Provider shall not use Customer Input to train or fine-tune its foundation models. | Source: 04-dpa-excerpt.md, Clause 5.1
6. SAFETY & ABUSE CLASSIFIER TRAINING CARVE-OUT: Notwithstanding Clause 5.1, Provider is permitted to use any requests retained under Clause 4.2 (flagged for suspected abuse) to train and evaluate its abuse and safety classifiers. | Source: 04-dpa-excerpt.md, Clause 5.2
7. SUB-PROCESSOR ENGAGEMENT: Provider may engage third-party sub-processors listed in Schedule 2—specifically a cloud hosting provider and a contracted content-review vendor—while remaining responsible for them. | Source: 04-dpa-excerpt.md, Clause 6.1
8. HUMAN & SUB-PROCESSOR ACCESS: Customer Input (and output of flagged requests) may be viewed/accessed by authorised Provider reviewers, Provider personnel, and Schedule 2 sub-processors (including the contracted content-review vendor) for service delivery (Clause 3) and suspected abuse review (Clause 4.2). | Source: 04-dpa-excerpt.md, Clauses 4.2 & 6.2
9. TECHNICAL SAFEGUARDS (ENCRYPTION): Data leaves Larkspur over TLS; Provider states industry-standard encryption in transit and at rest, while holding no credentials to Larkspur internal systems. | Source: 02-dataflow-diagram.md; 03-vendor-model-card.md ("Your data")

[NON-ENFORCEABLE / INFORMATIVE STATEMENTS — 03-vendor-model-card.md]
10. MODEL CARD TRAINING STATEMENT: Claims "We do not use your API inputs to train our foundation models," which omits the Clause 5.2 carve-out permitting training of abuse/safety classifiers on flagged inputs. | Source: 03-vendor-model-card.md ("Your data") vs. 04-dpa-excerpt.md, Clause 5.2
11. MODEL CARD LEGAL DISCLAIMER: Explicitly states "For the legal terms that apply to your account, refer to your agreement with Tessel AI," confirming the model card is non-binding marketing/technical documentation. | Source: 03-vendor-model-card.md ("Your data")
12. MODEL NON-TRUST BOUNDARY: Vendor warns that tessel-core-2 can be influenced by instructions appearing in text it is given and must not be relied upon as a security boundary. | Source: 03-vendor-model-card.md ("Safety and limitations")

[RESULTING RISK REGISTER ENTRIES CARRIED INTO 1-risk_register.md]
- MAP-01 (Third-Party Extended Retention, Secondary Classifier Training & Sub-Processor Human Review): Captures the gap between perceived zero-retention/no-training privacy and DPA Clauses 4.1 (30-day default retention), 4.2 (365-day abuse retention + human review), 5.2 (abuse/safety classifier training on flagged prompts/tool results), and 6.1–6.2 (access by external content-review vendor). Maps to OWASP LLM02:2025 (Sensitive Information Disclosure) & MITRE ATLAS AML.T0024 (Exfiltration via ML Inference API). Mitigated via Control F4 (Vendor addendum: zero retention and training opt-out, 3 eng-weeks).
- MAP-02 (Unscrubbed Reply-Side & Residual Account Data Exposure to External Logs/Reviewers): Addresses residual account identifiers (`customer_id`, `plan`, `invoice_total_cents`, `card_last4`) sent in every `model_call` (`05-sample-logs.jsonl`) and any regulated fields (`iban`, `billing_address`, `tax_id`) if scrubbing fails or leaks in outputs, which would persist for 30–365 days at Tessel AI and its content-review sub-processor without F2 and F4. Maps to OWASP LLM02:2025 & MITRE ATLAS AML.T0048 (Societal / Privacy Harm).
- GOV-02 (Misleading Compliance Assurance & Missing Regulatory Classification C-12): Records that C-05 ("DPA signed") and C-06 ("sub-processors approved") were marked "Yes" in `06-compliance-self-assessment.md` without flagging the 365-day retention, classifier training carve-out, or external content-review sub-processor exposure for regulated billing/financial data (C-12 = "No"). Maps to OWASP LLM02:2025 & MITRE ATLAS AML.T0048.
