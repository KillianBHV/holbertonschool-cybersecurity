# Risk Acceptance Memo - R7 (DDos)

* **Risk Statement**: This memo formally accepts the risk of website unavailability due to a Distributed Denial of Service (DDoS) attack during product launches.
* **Economic Rationale**: The current annualized financial exposure (ALE) is €8,000 (AV: €8,000, EF: 1, ARO: 1), which is significantly lower than the €24,000 annual cost of the proposed anti-DDoS control (Proposal B).
* **Risk Appetite Alignment**: This un-insured exposure of €8,000 strictly complies with Averline’s risk appetite, which tolerates individual annual exposures below €25,000.
* **Ownership & Review**: The Chief Financial Officer (CFO) is the designated owner of this risk, with a formal review scheduled for October 9, 2027.
* **Trigger Condition**: This decision will be reopened early if the annual rate of occurrence (ARO) exceeds 1 event per year.
