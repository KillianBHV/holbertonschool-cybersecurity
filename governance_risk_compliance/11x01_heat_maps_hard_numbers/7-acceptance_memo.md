# Risk Acceptance Memo - R7 (DDos)

* **Risk Statement**: This memo formally accepts the risk of website unavailability due to a Distributed Denial of Service (DDoS) attack during product launches.
* **Economic Rationale**: The current annualized financial exposure (ALE) is €8,000, which is significantly lower than the €24,000 annual cost of the proposed anti-DDoS control *(Proposal B)*.
* **Risk Appetite Alignment**: This un-insured exposure of €8,000 strictly complies with Averline's risk appetite, which tolerates individual exposures below €25,000.
* **Ownership & Review**: The Chief Financial Officer (CFO) is the designated owner of this risk, with a formal review scheduled within twelve months.
* **Trigger Condition**: This decision will be reopened early if the annual rate of occurence (ARO) exceeds ***one event per year*** or if the ***financial impact per downtime*** increases.
