* Vulnerable Endpoint: POST /promote
* Reachable flaw: The authentication handler fetches a custom authorization token from the incoming HTTP headers and compares it to a server-side environment variable. However, this environment variable was left completely unset (null) during the production deployment. 
* Consequence: By omitting the authentication header in the curl request, both sides of the comparison evaluate to null/empty. The check passes successfully (null == null), rendering the endpoint reachable without any credentials.
